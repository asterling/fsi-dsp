- Alert per SLA tier:

  - `ConsumerLagStandard`: warn at 10000, alert at 50000. Labels: `sla_tier=standard`
  - `ConsumerLagBestEffort`: warn at 100000, alert at 500000. Labels: `sla_tier=best-effort`
