<objective>
Build the cp_dr_mrc Ansible role for MRC observer promotion failover and failback.

Purpose: ADR-05 requires MRC failover via Confluent CLI (kafka-leader-election.sh) for RPO=0 scenarios. This role mirrors the cp_dr_mm2 structure but replaces connector management with leader election operations. UNCLEAN election promotes observers to leaders during failover; PREFERRED election rebalances leadership during failback.

Output: Complete cp_dr_mrc role with 6 task files, templates, molecule scenario, operator-facing playbooks, and unit tests.
</objective>
